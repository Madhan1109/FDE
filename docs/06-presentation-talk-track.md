# How to present FDE (talk track)

Use this as a 5–8 minute speaking script. Say it in your own words.

---

## Opening (30 seconds)

“Today I’m talking about the Forward Deployed Engineer role.  
In plain English: an FDE is an engineer who sits close to the customer, understands a real operational problem, and ships working software in that environment so the product creates value — not just a demo.”

---

## Why the role exists (45 seconds)

“Many products fail after the sale. The software is powerful, but the customer’s data, security rules, and workflows are different.  
Someone has to bridge that gap. That someone is the FDE.”

---

## What FDE does (60 seconds)

“An FDE discovers the mission, designs a thin first version, builds integrations, deploys, tests with real users, and measures outcome.  
Then they feed reusable lessons back to the core product team.”

---

## How it differs from nearby roles (60 seconds)

“Unlike a classic software engineer, an FDE optimizes for one customer outcome first.  
Unlike a solutions engineer, an FDE stays through deployment and adoption, not only the sales demo.  
Unlike support, an FDE builds systems that remove repeated pain.”

---

## Skills (45 seconds)

“You need strong coding, debugging, and systems thinking.  
You also need communication, scoping, and security awareness.  
For AI FDE roles, you need agents, tools, RAG, guardrails, and evaluation.”

---

## Example (60–90 seconds)

Use a real project as the example (for example an HR Chat Agent):

“If I were the FDE for an HR AI assistant, I would not stop at a chatbot demo.  
I would map the company’s leave workflow, connect to their employee records, add authentication, enforce privacy so one employee cannot see another’s data, deploy it for a pilot team, watch tool traces, and measure whether leave questions get resolved faster.”

---

## Closing (20 seconds)

“FDE success is simple to say and hard to do: customer outcomes in production, with a path to durable product value.”

---

## Likely audience questions + short answers

**Q: Is FDE just consulting?**  
A: Consulting often ends in advice. FDE ends in working software and ownership of delivery.

**Q: Do FDEs write production code?**  
A: Yes. That is the point.

**Q: Will FDE work disappear when product is mature?**  
A: The deepest custom work shrinks, but complex enterprise deployment and AI adaptation still need FDEs.

**Q: What is hardest?**  
A: Ambiguity — unclear owners, messy data, security constraints, and pressure to ship before the problem is fully known.
