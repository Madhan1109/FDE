# FDE vs other roles (plain English)

Use this when someone asks: “Isn’t that just Solutions Engineer / Support / SWE?”

## Quick comparison

| Role | Main job | Coding depth | Customer closeness | Output |
|------|----------|--------------|--------------------|--------|
| **Software Engineer (SWE)** | Build the core product | Very high | Usually low | Product features |
| **Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)** | Make product work for a specific customer | High | Very high | Working customer solution + feedback to product |
| **Solutions Engineer / Sales Engineer** | Win the deal with demos and technical answers | Medium | High (pre-sales) | Demos, PoCs, trust |
| **Solutions Architect** | Design the system shape | Medium–high | High | Architecture diagrams, plans |
| **Professional Services / Consultant** | Deliver a project | Mixed | High | Reports + sometimes delivery |
| **Customer Support / Success** | Keep customers healthy | Low–medium | High | Tickets resolved, adoption help |
| **DevOps / Platform** | Keep systems running | High (infra) | Low–medium | Reliable environments |
| **Product Manager** | Decide what to build | Low | Medium | Roadmap, priorities |

## SWE vs FDE

**SWE**
- Builds one product for many customers
- Works from specs / roadmap
- Success = feature shipped, tests green, scale

**FDE**
- Adapts one product for one customer mission
- Works from messy real requirements
- Success = customer outcome in production

Both write code. FDE code is often more integration-heavy and time-boxed.

## Solutions Engineer vs FDE

**Solutions Engineer**
- Mostly before/during sale
- Demo, answer questions, prove feasibility
- May build a PoC, then move to next deal

**FDE**
- Mostly after sale / during deployment
- Stays longer with one customer
- Turns PoC into something people use every day

## Architect vs FDE

**Architect** designs.  
**FDE** designs *and* implements under real constraints.

## Support vs FDE

**Support** reacts to problems.  
**FDE** builds the path so those problems stop happening.

## When companies use which title

- If the job is “help sales close deals” → often Solutions Engineer  
- If the job is “live with customer and ship production value” → FDE  
- If the job is “design cloud landing zone” → Solutions Architect  
- If the job is “build platform used by millions” → SWE

## How to choose your story

If you like:
- coding + ambiguity + customer conversations + fast shipping  
→ FDE fits.

If you like:
- deep systems, long-term product craft, less customer travel  
→ classic SWE fits.

If you like:
- presenting, demos, persuasion, lighter implementation  
→ Solutions Engineer fits.
